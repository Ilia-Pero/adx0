# Changes

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-25)

- A button at the bottom of the window looks for a newer version. The program does not do this by itself.
- The extension for Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge and Opera.
- Days and numbers in Belarusian and Kazakh on systems that do not know these languages.
- Lines of controls have enough contrast in both themes; scroll bars follow the dark theme.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-25)

- Kazakh and Belarusian: 14 languages. The installer for Windows does not know Kazakh; it is chosen at the top of the window.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-25)

- While blocking is on, the lists are downloaded again when the system starts, at most once a day. The program starts for a moment without a window and exits. A person can turn this off.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-25)

- Windows and Ubuntu name Ilia-Pero as the publisher. In 0.1.0 it was “github” by mistake.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- The program: lists of ad addresses in the hosts file, sites of one's own to block and to open.
- The Firefox extension: blocked addresses, hidden ad blocks, YouTube without ads.
- Regional lists.
- 12 languages. The language is chosen at the top of the window, in the installer for Windows and in the window of the extension.
